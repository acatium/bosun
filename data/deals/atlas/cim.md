# Project Atlas: Confidential Information Memorandum

Prepared by Woodgrove Bank, Capital Markets Advisory, for a limited number of prospective investors.
Wide World Importers and Woodgrove Bank are fictional companies used for demonstration only.

## The opportunity

Woodgrove Bank has been retained by the shareholders of Wide World Importers ("the Company") to explore
a sale of a majority stake. First-round, non-binding indications of interest are due on 14 November 2026.

## Company overview

Wide World Importers is a US mid-market logistics business founded in 2009 and headquartered in
Columbus, Ohio. It operates two segments:

- **Freight brokerage** (61% of revenue): asset-light truckload and less-than-truckload brokerage for
  around 1,400 shippers, served through a proprietary load-matching platform.
- **Dedicated contract logistics** (39% of revenue): 11 warehouses totalling 3.2 million square feet,
  under contracts averaging 4.1 years, mainly for consumer goods and industrial customers.

The Company employs 1,150 people.

## Investment highlights

1. Contract logistics revenue is 92% renewed over the last three years.
2. Brokerage gross margin has held between 14% and 16% through two freight cycles.
3. The load-matching platform lets one broker handle 38% more loads than the industry benchmark.
4. Management has identified a pipeline of five tuck-in acquisitions in the Midwest.

## Key risks

1. **Customer concentration.** The top five customers are 41% of revenue; the largest is 14%.
2. **Spot rate exposure.** About 35% of brokerage volume is priced on the spot market, so margins move
   with truckload rates.
3. **Driver and carrier availability.** Capacity tightness raised purchased transportation costs by 6%
   in 2024.
4. **Technology.** The load-matching platform runs on a codebase that needs re-platforming within three
   years; management estimates USD 6 to 8 million.
5. **Lease renewals.** Three warehouses (0.9 million square feet) have leases expiring in 2027.

## Process
